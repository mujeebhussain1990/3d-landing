# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript) + Tailwind, React Three Fiber, @react-three/drei, @react-three/postprocessing, GSAP ScrollTrigger, Lenis, custom GLSL shaders. Chosen by the user from the recommended award-level stack.

## Users

Potential clients (founders, product owners, enterprise IT/marketing leads) evaluating a software partner. They arrive to judge whether HashOne Global can be trusted with their product, then send a project inquiry.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for HashOne Global, a software development and digital solutions company. Success = a qualified visitor sends a project inquiry.

## Positioning

Tagline (from hashoneglobal.com): "We Build High-Performing Digital Solutions that Turn Ideas into Impact." Full-service delivery under one roof across five offices.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services (from hashoneglobal.com): mobile app development (iOS, Android, cross-platform); web development (portals, e-commerce, CMS); custom software (ERP, CRM, LMS); artificial intelligence (chatbots, generative AI, ML, computer vision); cloud services (migration, support, maintenance); digital marketing (SEO, social, paid ads, content); animation (2D, motion graphics, product demos); resource augmentation.

Offices: Florida (US), Dubai (UAE), Riyadh (KSA), Toronto (Canada), Karachi (Pakistan).
Contact: info@hashoneglobal.com.

## Evidence on Hand

Stated on hashoneglobal.com: 5.0 rating on Clutch; "100% confidential, NDA ready"; "24-hour response guarantee"; 100+ completed projects; 10+ years in business; 200+ experienced resources; 50+ countries served; Microsoft Gold, Shopify, Odoo, Zoho Advanced partner.

## Product Principles

1. Craft is the proof: the page itself demonstrates what the team can build.
2. Every section moves the visitor toward an inquiry.
3. Only real claims from the company's own site.
4. Fast and usable on mobile despite the 3D.
