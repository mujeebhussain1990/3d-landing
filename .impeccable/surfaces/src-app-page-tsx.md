# Surface: Home landing page

Mode: Persuade. Audience: potential clients choosing a software partner. Action: send a project inquiry (form + info@hashoneglobal.com). Proof: only stated facts from hashoneglobal.com (see PRODUCT.md). Build path: code-led (no image generation).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page itself turns an idea into a built thing. One glowing point becomes a swarm of particles that assembles, step by step, into each service's form. It refuses the default agency hero (headline + abstract blob + icon-card grid).

OWN-WORLD: HashOne brand theme (user-pinned 2026-10-07): navy-black ground #05070E, cyan #5FD4F4 particles and primary actions, logo blue #40A8DE particle sprinkle, pale cyan #BFF0FF globe arcs. Drenched fields: royal blue #1E56C8 ("rest of the build", post CTA) and cyan (contact). Plus Jakarta Sans 800 tight sentence-case display ("Impact." in cyan), Work Sans body. Pill buttons and chips, hairline-ruled rows.

STORY: Visitors see an idea become a product, understand the services through shapes they recognise, believe the scale through real stats and five offices, then send an inquiry.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The full-bleed canvas holds the idea point exploding into a particle swarm that reacts to the cursor. Bottom-left has a huge 3-line display headline "IDEAS / INTO / IMPACT". Bottom-right has the tagline, plus a lime "Start a project" slab and a scroll cue. A thin top nav holds the wordmark, links and 24h response note.

FORM: Idea→Impact particle assembly, my list position 3, seed a3494f92. Raises: from Agit-train, lime is rationed to the action wedge only. From Forge strike, there is one deliberate transformation per scroll step with measured numerals. From Phosphor terminal, there is one display face at total commitment.

Signature interaction: scroll-scrubbed GPU particle morph (idea → phone → browser → neural lattice → cloud → globe with 5 office beacons). Cursor repels particles. Lenis smooth scroll, GSAP ScrollTrigger timelines. Bloom + grain postprocessing. Reduced motion: static formed globe, no scrub.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
